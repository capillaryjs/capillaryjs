# Code of Conduct

This project expects contributors to behave professionally.

Disagreement, criticism, strong opinions, and technical debate are normal and welcome. Participants are expected to focus on the work, communicate honestly, and resolve disagreements constructively.

Participants must:

* act in good faith;
* assume good faith in others;
* give and receive direct, constructive criticism professionally;
* focus discussion on the project, the work, and the code rather than on personal characteristics, circumstances, or motives;
* avoid knowingly misrepresenting another person's words, actions, or contributions;
* not use project processes, including this Code of Conduct, to silence legitimate disagreement, retaliate against others, or gain leverage in unrelated disputes.

Disagreement, bluntness, mistakes, misunderstandings, or criticism are not in themselves misconduct.

These standards apply to project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and other spaces in which someone participates on behalf of the project.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation, or impose temporary or permanent bans when conduct materially violates these standards. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and circumstances.

Conduct concerns may be reported privately to `contact@sylwellsoftware.com`. Reports will be handled with appropriate discretion, and significant conflicts of interest should be avoided when deciding an outcome.

The purpose of this Code is to support productive collaboration, not to regulate ordinary interpersonal friction or enforce agreement.
